# Post Formulas — pick one per draft

Each formula is a proven shape for tying a news topic to a UGC creator POV. The Ideator should choose 1–2 candidate formulas per topic.

## 1. The Insider Translation
> Hook: a confident plain-English translation of what the news actually means for a small slice of the audience.

```
[News event] is going to hit [niche] before anyone else notices.
Here's why I'm already seeing it in [the work I do].
[2-3 lines of concrete evidence from the trenches]
[Implication for the reader: what to do, watch, or stop doing]
```

Best for: macro news (Fed, earnings, layoffs) → DTC creator budget impact.

## 2. The Contrarian Read
> Hook: take the obvious read of the news and reverse it.

```
Everyone is reading [headline] as [obvious take].
I think it's the opposite.
[Concrete reason from your day-to-day]
[The reframe and what it means]
```

Best for: hyped narratives where consensus is loud (AI spending, big tech moves).

## 3. The Pattern You're Already Seeing
> Hook: a small concrete observation from your work that *foreshadowed* the news.

```
[Specific observation from past N weeks]. I didn't think much of it.
Then today's news made it click: [the bigger pattern].
[1-2 lines connecting the dots]
[What to do about it]
```

Best for: when the news confirms a trend Ethan already feels in client briefs / pricing / engagement.

## 4. The Cost-of-Doing-Nothing
> Hook: name a thing brands are about to lose if they don't adapt.

```
If you're a [target reader] and you saw [news], you have ~[time window] before [consequence].
The brands that get this win: [behavior].
The ones that don't: [outcome].
[Single line CTA / save-bait]
```

Best for: news that creates urgency for DTC brand decision-makers.

## 5. The Story From The Floor
> Hook: open mid-scene with something you actually witnessed this week, then pull back to the news.

```
[A specific moment: a Slack message, a brief, a pricing call, a comment under a video.]
[What happened next.]
[The reveal: this is what [news event] looks like up close.]
[The line you want screenshotted.]
```

Best for: when a piece of news has a perfect real-world micro-version Ethan lived.

## Picking between them

| If the news is... | Use formula |
|---|---|
| Macro econ / Fed / earnings | 1 or 4 |
| Loud consensus narrative | 2 |
| A confirmation of a trend Ethan already sees | 3 |
| A vivid moment Ethan can match | 5 |
| Genuinely off-topic for UGC | None — skip the day |
