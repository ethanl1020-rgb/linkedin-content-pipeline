## PHASE 4 — COPYWRITER

### Final Post

26% of consumers prefer AI-generated content. Down from 60% in 2023. That number is your brief.

Two years ago brands were rushing to swap real creators for AI avatars. Cheaper. Faster. Infinite scale. Made complete sense on paper.

Then conversion dropped. Comment sections got quiet. The trust eroded.

Turns out consumers got very good, very fast, at spotting content where nobody had skin in the game.

The brands that held the line on real creators are quietly winning right now. Not out of sentiment — the data finally caught up.

No script. No avatar. Someone who'd actually use the thing.

That's still the brief.
