## FINAL POST

UGC rates just fell 44% year over year. I raised mine.

Average deliverable is sitting at $198 now. New creators flooded the market. AI tools dropped the floor. Brands had more options — rates compressed. The math makes sense.

But the brands still running their *best-performing* ads? They're not buying at $198.

Cheap UGC doesn't underperform because it's cheap. It underperforms because of the brief-to-execution gap.

The creator takes the brief literally. Shoots something technically fine. Nothing in it sounds like the actual customer. Converts at 0.8x. Brand decides UGC doesn't work.

A creator who can translate a brand voice into 15 real seconds didn't get cheaper. The market just made it easier to tell them apart from the ones who can't.

When that $198 test batch comes back flat, some brands get it.

Those are my clients.

Commoditization is a filter, not a threat.
