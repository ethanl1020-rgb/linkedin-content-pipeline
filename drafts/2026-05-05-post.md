AI UGC tools promised volume at zero drama: no creator management, no rates, no revision cycles. Brands scaled it fast.

The early performance numbers looked fine.

What didn't show up in the dashboard: consumers are detecting synthetic content faster than marketers expected. Not from a disclaimer. From something they couldn't name — a read they got in the first two seconds.

The trust damage isn't a click-through problem. It shows up in repurchase, in comment sections, in DMs that ask "wait, was that person real?"

The brands still converting on short-form? Real people. In real spaces. Saying the thing in a way their customers actually say it.

Authenticity was never a value. It was always a strategy.
